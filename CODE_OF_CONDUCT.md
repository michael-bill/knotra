# Community conduct

Knotra welcomes contributions from people of all backgrounds and experience levels. Treat
contributors and users with respect. Critique ideas and code, explain disagreements with evidence,
and give people room to learn.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, personal attacks, doxxing and unwanted sexual
attention are unacceptable. Do not publish another person's private information without permission.
These expectations apply to issues, pull requests and other project spaces.

Report concerns privately to a project maintainer. Maintainers may edit or remove content, close
discussions or restrict participation to keep project spaces safe. They should explain decisions,
protect reporters' privacy and apply these expectations consistently. If a maintainer is involved,
contact another maintainer where possible.
